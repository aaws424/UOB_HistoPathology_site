## About this Slide
The spleen is a vital organ in the immune system. This slide shows the distinct white pulp and red pulp areas. 

- **White Pulp**: Lymphoid tissue surrounding the central arteries.
- **Red Pulp**: Consists of splenic sinusoids and splenic cords (Cords of Billroth).

### Technical Metadata
- **Format**: BigTIFF (.btif)
- **Stain**: Hematoxylin and Eosin (H&E)
- **Scanning**: 40x Magnification
