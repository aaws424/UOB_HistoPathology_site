# Virtual Pathology Atlas

Welcome to the Digital Histopathology Atlas at the University of Leeds (Modernized).

This website allows you to explore Whole Slide Images (WSI) in high resolution directly in your browser.

## Getting Started

1. Use the sidebar on the left to navigate through different pathology sections.
2. Select a technical or disease area to view related slides.
3. Use your mouse wheel to zoom in/out and drag to pan across the tissue samples.

![Demo](https://virtualpathology.leeds.ac.uk/images/logo.png)

> [!NOTE]
> This atlas is powered by MkDocs, OpenSlide, and OpenSeadragon.
